Tech Radar: the business process

How a technology goes from a web page to Tata's leaders, who does each step, where people decide, and what a user can do at each step. As it runs on 21 Oct 2026; numbers are today's settings.

9Publish an editiona person; no AI
  • Freezes the placements, Points of View and assumptions as they stand
  • Refused while an assessment or Points of View are being written
You can
  • Publish the edition, named and dated (Briefing)
  • Check the assumptions first (Setup › Assumptions)
5Fill the gap before scoringa person, on Briefing and the technology's page
You can
  • AIFind evidence: 1 web search, 5 results (20 searches a day)
  • Add a link you already know
  • Possibly nascent: Yes (assess despite thin evidence) or No
  • Review catalog import matches: accept or reject
  • Switch it off, or wait for the next collection
7Settle what's opena person, with Tata; the technology's fabric tab
You can
  • Score a measure by hand, or accept the gap (with a reason)
  • Enter Tata's figure: disruption threat, serviceable capture
  • Accept or reject suggested use cases
  • Override the priority (with a reason)
  • Tick the blockers that apply
  • Record the step reached on the path to scale
  • Record the owner, next action and what's committed
  • Enter finance figures (from Proof of concept)
2Collect and read pagesautomatic, once started
  • Crawls each switched-on source: up to 60 pages per source, 5 crawlers at once; unchanged pages skipped
  • AI1 call per new page: a summary, the findings (events with a quote) and the technologies it names
  • Long pages read in parts
You can
  • Watch it, or stop it (Operations)
  • Add sources, switch them on or off (Setup › Sources)
  • Change what counts as a finding (Setup › Signal list), then Check again
  • Read the findings (Briefing)
3Organize technologiesautomatic, after each collection
  • Code merges spellings of the same name
  • AIGroups new names into technologies (up to 4,000 names per call)
  • Links each page to its technologies
You can
  • Organize now (Operations)
  • Rename a technology, merge others into it, switch it off (its page › Manage)
  • Add a technology by hand (Technologies)
4Classify each technologyautomatic, after each collection
  • AI1 call per technology, reading up to 10 of its pages; 6 at a time
  • Commercial availability, domain and sub-domain, TRL, how solid the evidence is, possibly nascent; each with its reason and pages
You can
  • Move to another commercial availability or domain; add to or take out of the catalog (Technologies › tick › Move to…)
  • Change TRL or sub-domain (its page)
  • Import the catalog file and review it (Setup › Import)
  • Accept, merge or rename proposed sub-domains (Setup › Classification)
6Assessa person starts it; then automatic
  • AIPer technology: 1 call (which fabrics it matters to; market, growth, maturity) + 1 call per relevant fabric (7 measures; up to 2 use cases with their levers and capital; blockers the pages show). About 3 calls each, 6 technologies at a time
  • Code works out the three scores, the priority (P1–P6) and the horizon
You can
  • Assess the ready ones, or Resume a stopped run (Briefing, Operations); the cost shows first
  • Assess one technology (its page)
  • Change measures, weights and cut-points (Setup › Scoring method)
8Write Points of Viewa person starts it; then automatic
  • AI1 call per technology, from its assessment; 6 at a time
  • Explains, never scores; every claim cites a numbered page
You can
  • Write all that wait, or Rewrite one (Points of View)
  • Read it, open Why on any score, print or save as PDF
10Read the editionTata's leaders, at their own address
  • The grid by fabric and stage; best bets on the frontier
  • Each technology's Point of View, owner state and evidence
  • The assumptions in force
They can
  • Switch fabric, stage, and the Growth or Defence lens
  • Open a Point of View, print it
  • Nothing here changes data: it's read-only
end
